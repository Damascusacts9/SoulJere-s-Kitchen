        Method

          Don't. Don't cook the week-pot if you want green kale on day four. Don't default to half a warehouse slab of bacon for 5–6 bowls. Don't skip the splash-and-scrape. Don't add a gallon of water — only enough to cover the potatoes. Don't blend after the kale and cream. Don't boil the cream. Don't salt before you taste.
          Leftovers — Zuppa. Off heat, into shallow containers. Fridge within 2 hours. Eat in 3–4 days for safety; kale is tired by day 3. Freeze 2–3 months — cream may split; whisk on a gentle reheat. Reheat soup to a boil. Do not reheat the whole pot every night. Overnight on the stove is trash.

          Cool it like you mean it

          A deep stockpot in the fridge stays warm in the middle for hours.

          • Lid loose until steam drops, then seal.
          • Label the date. Day 1 is cook day.

          How long

          Quality dies before safety sometimes. If you are guessing, it goes in the can. Do not taste to decide.

          Reheat

          • Soups and gravies: bring to a boil.
          • Microwave: cover, stir, stand, check the center.
          • Do not reheat in a slow cooker.
          • Back in the fridge within 2 hours of that reheat.
          Rice note. Cooked rice can carry Bacillus cereus spores. Cool rice dishes fast. Do not leave the skillet on the stove "for later."
